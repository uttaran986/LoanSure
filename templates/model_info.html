How the NLP pipeline works
1
Form → text

Structured form data is converted to a natural language sentence describing the applicant's full financial profile

2
Tokenization

Text is tokenized using BERT's WordPiece tokenizer into up to 128 subword tokens with [CLS] and [SEP] markers

3
BERT encoding

DistilBERT processes all tokens simultaneously with multi-head self-attention across 6 transformer layers

4
Classification

The [CLS] embedding passes through a linear head → softmax → Approved/Rejected with probability scores
